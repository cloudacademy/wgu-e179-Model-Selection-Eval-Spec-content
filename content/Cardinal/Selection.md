# Cardinal Trust Financial

A customer makes a payment. The bank has a fraction of a second to score it, but a fraud analyst may need to explain the flag much later. Consumer Payments wants to introduce transaction scoring on its existing private cloud. A missed case can cost the bank money. An unnecessary flag can delay a genuine purchase and take time from the payments desk.

You will advise the payments security team, evaluate a classifier on synthetic transaction records, and review Veriscan Analytics’ proposal for FraudGuard AI. The bank wants to know what staff could act on and what they could explain to an auditor.

Your chosen client applies to all three independent tasks.
