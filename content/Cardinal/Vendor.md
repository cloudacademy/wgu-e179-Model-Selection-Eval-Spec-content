# FraudGuard AI | Veriscan Analytics
## Customer briefing, edition 3
FraudGuard AI flags transactions as they arrive. Veriscan reports 99.2% detection accuracy in an internal evaluation. The published figure covers transactions for which analysts had closed the investigation within seven days. The display reports the highest monthly result from the release qualification period. Analysts assigned a default legitimate disposition to payments without a case referral.

Veriscan supplies monthly portfolio totals. The analyst interface displays a score and case number. The evaluation corpus, training records, model weights and architecture are retained by Veriscan. The company supplies a version label in quarterly summaries. A third-party contractor labels development data. An external scoring partner serves the underlying engine through its own API. That partner sets its maintenance windows and distributes engine revisions to all subscribers.

The standard agreement offers a next-business-day service desk. Changes to case-scoring behavior are described in quarterly customer updates. Detailed scoring records remain on the vendor service for 30 days. Availability figures cover the Veriscan application during staffed support hours.
