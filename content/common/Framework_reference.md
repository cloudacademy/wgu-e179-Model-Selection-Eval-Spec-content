# Governance reference
## NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0
**Govern** establishes policies, roles, accountability and a risk-aware culture across the AI lifecycle. **Map** establishes the context of use, affected parties and potential impacts. **Measure** selects and applies methods to evaluate, analyze and track identified risks. **Manage** prioritizes risks and allocates actions and resources for treatment, monitoring and response. The functions can operate together. For this assessment, assign one primary function per risk as required by the task.

National Institute of Standards and Technology. (2023). *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, §§ 5.1–5.4. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1

## EU Artificial Intelligence Act
The following documentation categories are used for the procurement exercise. The regulation assigns obligations according to role, system classification and application dates. The client brief does not determine legal classification.

| Documentation category | Scope of the reference |
| --- | --- |
| Technical documentation | Article 11 and Annex IV describe a technical file for a high-risk system, prepared before placement on the market or putting into service and kept current. It describes the system, design and development, monitoring, capabilities and limitations, and relevant testing information. |
| Evidence of risk management processes | Article 9 describes a documented, iterative lifecycle process to identify and evaluate risks, adopt controls, test and review them. Annex IV also calls for a description of that system. |
| Declaration of conformity | Article 47 and Annex V specify a written EU declaration for each high-risk system, identifying the provider and system and declaring conformity under the provider's responsibility. It is a distinct document from test reports. |

European Parliament and Council of the European Union. (2024). *Regulation (EU) 2024/1689*, arts. 9, 11, 47 and annexes IV–V. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng
