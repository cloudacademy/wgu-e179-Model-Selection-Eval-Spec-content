# SentinelIQ | Threadline Security
## Customer briefing, edition 3
SentinelIQ prioritizes incoming security alerts. Threadline reports an 85% reduction in triage time at customer deployments. Participating customers nominated their fastest queue after rollout. The comparison period used the former manual routing workflow. Cases awaiting specialist review were excluded from the timing calculation.

The analyst view supplies a priority and a ticket identifier. Threadline reports aggregate queue times to customers. The training data, model architecture, trained weights and retraining schedule are proprietary. Threadline publishes no false-positive or false-negative rates. The classification engine is licensed from another AI provider. A separate integration partner maintains the connector that sends alerts to the scoring service and manages its release schedule.

The scoring service runs at the provider's endpoint. During an endpoint outage the connector queues events until the service returns. The standard subscription provides monthly release notes and retains classification events for 30 days. Third-party updates enter the shared service during the provider's scheduled maintenance window.
